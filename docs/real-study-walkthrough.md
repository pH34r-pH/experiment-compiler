## Reconstructing the interpretation from retained bytes

The frozen protocol compares the same `unit_hypersphere_depth3` model condition and initial-state fingerprints for seeds **17, 31 and 47**. Four reviewed matrix weights receive native PyTorch Muon; remaining parameters use matched AdamW. Only the treatment arm was executed: the reference is the frozen historical AdamW result, not a fresh measured reference arm. Seed 17 had already been observed during infrastructure qualification before the three-seed analysis was frozen; the protocol and decision retain that chronology.

Raw treatment trajectories and reference trajectories retain per-document NLL and original-byte denominators at updates **0, 8, 16, 32, 64, 96 and 128**. The primary endpoint is paired trapezoidal AULC difference, Muon minus historical AdamW; final byte-normalized NLL is secondary. For each of **eight source documents**, first average the paired difference over the three seeds, then use **65,536 deterministic document-bootstrap replicates**, seed **12,365,536**, for two-sided percentile 95% intervals. The statistical units are eight documents, not 24 independent seed/document pairs. The frozen **0.01 nats per original byte** value remains a materiality reference.

The source decision records primary +0.01620 nats/original byte (interval +0.01486 to +0.01773), favoring historical AdamW, and secondary −0.02266 (interval −0.02518 to −0.01903), favoring Muon. It calls the overall comparison **mixed/inconclusive**, with no optimizer-superiority, training-cost or cost-efficiency claim. The run stops at update 128; official test data stayed sealed. No fresh AdamW timing comparison, energy measurement, update-256 continuation or later-convergence conclusion exists.

[DSL #561](https://github.com/pH34r-pH/domain-scaling-lab/pull/561) adds retained-input offline reanalysis; [DSL #343](https://github.com/pH34r-pH/domain-scaling-lab/issues/343)/[#397](https://github.com/pH34r-pH/domain-scaling-lab/issues/397) retain the source compatibility review. This walkthrough preserves that unresolved boundary and does not revise the source decision or certify a causal optimizer comparison.
